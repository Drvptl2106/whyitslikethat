What ACTUALLY happens when you hit send on WhatsApp 👇

1️⃣ Your phone encrypts the message with a key only your friend's phone can unlock (end-to-end encryption)
2️⃣ It travels to WhatsApp's servers over a connection your phone keeps open → ✓ one grey tick
3️⃣ The server can't read it, it only knows who it's for
4️⃣ Friend offline? The server holds it for up to 30 days
5️⃣ Their phone comes online → message pushed down + decrypted → ✓✓ two grey ticks
6️⃣ They open the chat → a tiny read receipt flies back → blue ticks

So if it's blue and there's no reply… not a tech problem 😅
