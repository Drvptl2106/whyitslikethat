You buy a 1TB laptop. It shows 931GB. Did they scam you? 👀

Nope, there's a reason 👇

💾 Drive makers count in decimal: 1 TB = 1,000,000,000,000 bytes
🖥️ Windows counts in binary: 1 "GB" = 1,073,741,824 bytes (2³⁰)
➗ 1 trillion ÷ 1024 ÷ 1024 ÷ 1024 = 931.3, which Windows still labels "GB"

The gap grows with size: ~2% at KB, ~7% at GB, ~10% at TB.

⚖️ It went to court: Western Digital settled in 2006, Seagate offered refunds in 2007.
📖 The binary unit has a proper name, the "gibibyte" (GiB). Almost nobody uses it.
🍎 macOS has counted in decimal since 2009, so the same drive shows 1 TB on a Mac.
